CLINICAL CHATBOT GOOGLE SHEETS AUTH FIX

Problem
-------
The chatbot had its own service_account.json and database.py preferred that
chatbot-local key over the project-root credential. The two files are different
Google service-account keys for the same account. The chatbot-local key is being
rejected by Google with:
    invalid_grant: Invalid JWT Signature.

Fix
---
clinical_chatbot/database.py now prefers:
    IHS/service_account.json
and only falls back to:
    IHS/clinical_chatbot/service_account.json
when the root credential is not present.

This keeps the chatbot on the same Google credential used by the main SMART CDSS
registration/login flow. No UI, chatbot behavior, models, routes, or other project
features were changed.

After updating, fully stop the old Streamlit/FastAPI processes before restarting
the app so the stale chatbot process does not keep the old credential in memory.
