Logged-in patient linking update

Changes:
- Brain Stroke Diagnosis now automatically uses the logged-in user's name, age, and gender.
- Heart Disease Diagnosis now automatically uses the logged-in user's age and sex and displays the linked account identity.
- General Diagnostics now automatically uses the logged-in user's patient ID, name, age, and gender.
- Linked identity fields are read-only so an assessment cannot accidentally be recorded for a different person.
- No model, prediction logic, interface colors, or layout styling was changed.

Users should complete their Profile first so age and gender are available.
